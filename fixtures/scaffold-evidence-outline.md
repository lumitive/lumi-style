# The outline the release evidence renders from

It exists so `scaffold-render` grades a deck that CAN carry what the scaffold
emits for a declared analytical move. The command it feeds used to pass no
outline at all, so the browser gate rendered a document in which the element
under test could not appear — a measurement that succeeded where the thing
measured was absent, which is this package's own FM-24 one layer out.

## Where the spending goes

- Adoption rises with support hours, up to a point
  analysis: correlate | finding: adoption tracks support hours to a plateau | implication: Past the plateau more support stops buying adoption.
- Where the product stands against its two nearest rivals
  analysis: position | finding: broad and shallow against both | implication: Breadth is this period's order, depth is the next one's.
- What the shift costs, step by step
  analysis: bridge | finding: three moves account for the whole change | implication: Two of the three are ours to make.
