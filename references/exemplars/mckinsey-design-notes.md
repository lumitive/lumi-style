# What the real decks do — original design notes from the exemplar study

> Part of lumi-style. Derived by studying 18 publicly hosted McKinsey
> presentations (the study set and its provenance: `external/SOURCES.md`;
> the PDFs themselves are not committed). Everything below is original
> analysis of recurring devices, written to be executed against — each note
> names the local study page that exemplifies it, so a composition session
> can pull the page up beside the work.

*Serves: **GOAL**.* · id `EX-1`

## The ten devices that recur across firms' pages

1. **The action title is the finding, and its key phrase is bold inline.**
   Two lines maximum, sentence case, with the load-bearing noun phrase
   bolded mid-sentence ("Some **financial markets** in Europe are deep,
   but…"). Below it, a quiet **unit line** names the measure and year
   ("Financial depth 2017¹, %"). A kicker ABOVE the title, in a second
   colour, scopes the page ("Base Case: Fixed Cost of USO").
   *(Study pages: mck-21 p4, mck-01 p14.)*
2. **One exhibit dominates every content page — 50 to 70 percent of it.**
   The page is composed AROUND its exhibit, not decorated with one. The
   USPS cost page wraps four content quadrants around a central national
   map that itself carries the argument's summary in a callout at its
   centre. *(mck-01 p14, mck-23 p17.)*
3. **One hue family, tinted for composition; the second hue is a second
   theme.** Stacked bars run three tints of one blue; a page with two
   thematic halves gives each half one hue (blue engagement / orange
   agility) and keeps them pure. *(mck-21 p4, mck-26 p6.)*
4. **The subject is highlighted inside the data.** France and Germany —
   the page's subjects — are set in accent IN the row labels of a ten-row
   chart; everyone else stays ink. The reader finds the story without an
   arrow. *(mck-21 p4.)*
5. **Benchmarks are drawn into the chart, never narrated beside it.** A
   dashed vertical with an anchor marker sits IN the bar field; the gap to
   it IS the finding. *(mck-21 p4.)*
6. **Every quantified thing carries its number, and scale rides the
   heading.** Row values right-aligned at the bar end; a heading's noun
   carries its magnitude in parentheses — "Post Offices (36,500 Post
   Offices, Stations and Branches¹)". *(mck-01 p14.)*
7. **Icons and small images are identifiers and evidence, not decoration.**
   Flags identify countries in rows; small photos identify the four cost
   blocks; a full-bleed satellite image IS the exhibit on a land-market
   page, with the title in a left rail over it. *(mck-21 p4, mck-01 p14,
   mck-23 p17.)*
8. **Scope flags are printed on the page.** "NOT EXHAUSTIVE" sits at the
   top right of a strategy table — the page declares its own limits where
   the reader is standing. *(mck-15 p10.)*
9. **Framework pages are tables with scored criteria.** Four strategies as
   stadium-labelled rows, columns for description, worked examples, and TWO
   assessment criteria (time, difficulty) — the analysis is visible as a
   scoring, not a list. *(mck-15 p10.)*
10. **The record band closes every page**: superscripted footnotes above a
    full-width source strip ("SOURCE: USPS FY 2009 10-K"), page number at
    the right. *(All study pages.)*

## What this changes for a LUMI page

The waterline and the palette stay LUMI's (brand.md is identity, not up for
imitation). What the study corrects is **energy and dominance**: a LUMI page
that passes every gate can still be quiet in a way none of these pages are —
the exhibit under half the page, the subject unhighlighted, the benchmark in
prose, the numbers beside the geometry rather than in it. Compose to the ten
devices above with LUMI's own tokens: the accent ladder supplies the tint
family (device 3), `--lime` marks the subject or the event (device 4), the
`.band`/`.key` furniture carries device 6's scale-on-heading, and D25's
terms line already is device 10. Before composing a page, name the study
page it follows — the benchmark review (eval-rubric) reads the pair side by
side.

## The calibration codicil — devices validated on our own accepted pages

*Serves: **GOAL**.* · id `EX-2`

Two LUMI pages reached the owner's "this page is there" through iterative
calibration (2026-08: the evidence page, six versions; the market 2x2, one),
and the moves that carried them are part of the exemplar standard:

1. **One benchmark line through every measure.** Measures normalised to
   attainment of the written standard share one dashed line; three little
   charts with three little targets is display, one line everything must
   reach is analysis. (Device 5's generalisation.)
2. **A stat block is number-top**: the figure first at display size,
   the explanation under it in support ink, and colour spent by importance —
   the page's one key number in accent, the rest in text ink.
3. **A caption is a number and a name.** Everything else the old captions
   carried (tool names, per-figure source trails in a genre whose sourcing
   lives at document level) read as machine-added apparatus. External
   evidence is the exception and states its source with the claim.
4. **Reader-outcome lanes** (AR-5 drawn): driver → named evidence → gain,
   left to right, one lane per chain.
5. **Row labels at title weight.** A chart's row names are read before its
   bars; 17px bold ink, with the quiet detail a size below.
